# CUSTOM.md

This file is yours. Patina seeds it once and never overwrites it.

Add any always-on instructions you want your AI assistant to follow in every session. For example:

- Preferred communication style or tone
- Context your assistant should always have (tools you use, recurring projects, personal preferences)
- Things your assistant should always or never do in this patina

Your instructions here apply on top of the main context file — they are not replaced by updates.
